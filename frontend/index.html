Our Story

The Beginning

How Swamy Bakery Was Born

In 2000, Mr. Thangadurai opened a small 200 sq ft shop in Monday Market, Kanniyakumari, with just one fryer and an unwavering belief in quality snacks.

His philosophy: use only the best ingredients, treat every customer like family, and never compromise on freshness.

Today, with three locations and a thriving online presence, we still use the same original recipes that made people fall in love with us.

Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Swamy Bakery, we take immense pride in the quality and freshness of our products. Due to the perishable nature of food items, we have a strict policy regarding returns and refunds.

1. Returns

We do not accept returns for any food items once they have been delivered successfully. This is to ensure strict hygiene and food safety standards are maintained for all our customers.

2. Refunds & Replacements

Refunds or replacements will ONLY be considered under the following circumstances:

  • The product delivered is fundamentally damaged, stale, or completely unfit for consumption.
  • The packaging was severely compromised during transit resulting in damaged goods.
  • You received the wrong item(s) in your order.

3. How to Request a Refund or Replacement

If you face any of the issues mentioned above, you must contact us within 24 hours of delivery. To process your request, we require:

  • Your Order ID.
  • Clear photographs/videos of the damaged product and the packaging.
  • A brief description of the issue.

Please send this information to swamybakery3@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +91 9080729355.

4. Processing of Refunds

Once your claim is received and verified by our team, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund/replacement.

  • If approved for a replacement, a fresh product will be dispatched at no extra cost.
  • If approved for a refund, the amount will be processed back to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

5. Order Cancellations

Orders can only be cancelled if they have not yet been dispatched from our facility. Once an order has been handed over to our courier partner, it cannot be cancelled. To request a cancellation, please contact us immediately after placing the order.
