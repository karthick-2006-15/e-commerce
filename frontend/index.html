Our Story

From Kanyakumari to every home — every snack, every sweet carries a piece of our heart.

The Beginning

How Swamy Bakery Was Born

In 2000, Mr. Thangadurai opened a small 200 sq ft shop in Monday Market, Kanniyakumari, with just one fryer and an unwavering belief in quality snacks.

His philosophy: use only the best ingredients, treat every customer like family, and never compromise on freshness.

Today, with three locations and a thriving online presence, we still use the same original recipes that made people fall in love with us.
